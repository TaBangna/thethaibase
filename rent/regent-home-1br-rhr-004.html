About this unit

A brand-new, never-occupied one-bedroom unit in Tower D, facing east, with a separate enclosed kitchen that opens onto the balcony. Fully furnished: pocket-spring mattress, large sofa, 55-inch Google TV, front-loading washing machine, Stiebel water heater and 100% blackout curtains.
