Who it suits

Singles and couples who want plenty of shared space and newer facilities at a lower rent than the BTS-front buildings.

Not ideal for

  • Renters who want the shortest possible walk to the BTS
  • Pet owners

Good to know

  • Some property portals list Ideo O2 at 0.9 km from BTS Bang Na. The actual walk is about 500–650 m depending on the building; there is also a shuttle.

FAQ

How much is rent at Ideo O2?

As of October 2026, typical asking rents for a 12-month lease are: studio ฿11,000–฿14,000 (typically ฿12,000); 1 bedroom ฿11,000–฿16,000 (typically ฿14,500); 2 bedrooms ฿19,500–฿28,000 (typically ฿24,000) per month.

How far is Ideo O2 from BTS Bang Na?

About 500–650 m (7–9 minutes' walk) to BTS Bang Na, depending on the building, plus a building shuttle.

Are pets allowed at Ideo O2?

Not allowed unless approved by the building's juristic office. Most owners do not accept pets.

Ideo O2 or The Coast Bangkok?

Ideo O2 has more shared space and lower rents; The Coast is closer to the BTS, with a walkway and shops on site, and has larger units.
