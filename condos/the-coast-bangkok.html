The Coast Bangkok

A two-tower condo right next to BTS Bang Na and the BITEC exhibition centre, with a small shopping mall at its base.

Nearest BTS
BTS Bang Na
Distance
About 100 m to BTS Bang Na

Who it suits

Commuters who want the BTS at the door, and families who want a pool and play area in the building.

Unit types

1, 2 and 3 bedrooms, including duplex layouts (roughly 30–150 sq m)

Facilities

  • Indoor and outdoor saltwater pools
  • Gym and library
  • Rooftop and sky gardens
  • Children's playground
  • Shops and restaurants on site
  • Key-card access, CCTV, 24-hour security
  • Parking

Nearby

  • BITEC exhibition centre — next door
  • Central Bangna and Mega Bangna for shopping
  • Berkeley International School — about 1 km
  • Sikarin and Thainakarin hospitals
