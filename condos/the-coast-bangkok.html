The Coast Bangkok เดอะ โคสต์ แบงค็อก

A two-tower condo a short walk from BTS Bang Na, next to the BITEC exhibition centre, with the area's largest units and a small shopping mall at its base.

Who it suits

Families and couples who want a 2 or 3-bedroom unit within walking distance of the BTS, with a pool and play area in the building.

Not ideal for

  • Tight budgets: rents are the highest of the five buildings
  • Pet owners
  • Renters who want a brand-new building

Good to know

  • A walkway links the project to BTS Bang Na, so the walk to the station is short and easy in the rain.
  • This is the only building of the five with 3-bedroom and duplex units.

Unit types

1, 2 and 3 bedrooms, including duplex layouts (roughly 30–130 sq m)

Facilities

  • Indoor and outdoor saltwater pools
  • Gym, sauna and library
  • Rooftop and sky gardens
  • Children's playground
  • Shops and restaurants on site
  • Key-card access, CCTV, 24-hour security
  • Parking

Nearby

  • BITEC exhibition centre — next door
  • Central Bangna and Mega Bangna for shopping
  • Berkeley International School — about 1 km
  • Sikarin and Thainakarin hospitals

FAQ

How much is rent at The Coast Bangkok?

As of October 2026, typical asking rents for a 12-month lease are: 1 bedroom ฿12,000–฿14,500 (typically ฿13,000); 2 bedrooms ฿20,000–฿40,000 (typically ฿28,000); 3 bedrooms ฿55,000–฿80,000 (typically ฿60,000) per month.

How far is The Coast Bangkok from BTS Bang Na?

About 300 m to BTS Bang Na.

Are pets allowed at The Coast Bangkok?

Not allowed unless approved by the building's juristic office. Most owners do not accept pets.

Is The Coast Bangkok connected to BTS Bang Na?

Yes. A walkway links the project to BTS Bang Na, about 300 m away.

Does The Coast Bangkok have 2- and 3-bedroom units?

Yes. It has 1, 2 and 3-bedroom units, including duplex layouts, from roughly 30 to 130 sq m.
