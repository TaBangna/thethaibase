Who it suits

Single professionals who want a small, easy-to-manage unit near the BTS at the lowest rent in the area.

Not ideal for

  • Anyone who needs space: most units are 22–30 sq m
  • Pet owners

Good to know

  • Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastgate and Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastpoint are two different buildings right next to each other. Listings and AI answers often mix them up, so check the building name on any listing.

FAQ

How much is rent at Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastgate?

As of October 2026, typical asking rents for a 12-month lease are: studio ฿9,000–฿11,000 (typically ฿10,000); 1 bedroom ฿10,000–฿15,000 (typically ฿13,000) per month.

How far is Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastgate from BTS Bang Na?

About 300 m (5 minutes' walk) to BTS Bang Na.

Are pets allowed at Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastgate?

Not allowed unless approved by the building's juristic office. Most owners do not accept pets.

Is Ideo Mobi Eastgate the same as Ideo Mobi Eastpoint?

No. They are two separate Ananda Development buildings next to each other on Sukhumvit Road. Eastgate was completed in 2016 and has studios and 1-bedrooms; Eastpoint was completed in 2020 and also has duplexes and 2-bedrooms.
