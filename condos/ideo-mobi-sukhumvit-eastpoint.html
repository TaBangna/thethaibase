Who it suits

Professionals and couples who want a newer building on the BTS, with layouts from studio to 2 bedrooms.

Not ideal for

FAQ

How much is rent at Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastpoint?

As of October 2026, typical asking rents for a 12-month lease are: studio ฿13,000–฿15,000 (typically ฿14,000); 1 bedroom ฿13,000–฿20,000 (typically ฿17,000); 1-bedroom duplex ฿25,000–฿27,000 (typically ฿25,000); 2 bedrooms ฿28,000–฿35,000 (typically ฿30,000) per month.

How far is Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastpoint from BTS Bang Na?

About 250 m to BTS Bang Na; BTS Bearing also within walking distance.

Are pets allowed at Ideo Mobi Sukhumvit Eastpoint?

Not allowed unless approved by the building's juristic office. Most owners do not accept pets.

Is Ideo Mobi Eastpoint the same as Ideo Mobi Eastgate?

No. They are two separate Ananda Development buildings next to each other on Sukhumvit Road. Eastpoint (2020) is newer and has layouts up to 2 bedrooms; Eastgate (2016) has studios and 1-bedrooms at lower rents.
