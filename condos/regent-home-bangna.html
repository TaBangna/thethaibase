Regent Home Bangna รีเจ้นท์ โฮม บางนา

A new four-tower condo on Sanphawut Road, around a landscaped courtyard. Every unit is a 28 sq m one-bedroom with a separate bedroom: more privacy than a studio at studio rents. Home to a mix of Thai and international residents.

Who it suits

Singles and couples on a budget who want a separate bedroom rather than a studio, and renters working at BITEC or the Bang Na–Trat industrial estates.

Not ideal for

  • Anyone who needs more than 28 sq m: every unit is the same size
  • Pet owners
  • People who dislike very large projects: there are about 5,000 units

Good to know

  • Some listings advertise Regent Home Bangna as 350 m from BTS Bang Na. The actual walk is about 0.7 km, around 10 minutes, and varies by tower.

Unit types

Compact 1 bedrooms of 28 sq m in four layouts, each with a separate bedroom, balcony and kitchen area

Facilities

  • Lap pool and sky garden on the 4th floor
  • Gym, yoga studio and boxing room
  • Kids' play room
  • Sky-view co-working space on the 32nd floor
  • Ground-floor courtyard garden and a private lobby for each tower
  • 17 shop units within the project
  • Key-card access, floor-locked lifts, CCTV, 24-hour security
  • Parking

FAQ

How much is rent at Regent Home Bangna?

As of October 2026, typical asking rents for a 12-month lease are: 1 bedroom ฿9,500–฿13,000 (typically ฿11,000) per month.

How far is Regent Home Bangna from BTS Bang Na?

About 0.7 km (10 minutes' walk) to BTS Bang Na, via a rear gate and canal-side path.

Are pets allowed at Regent Home Bangna?

Not allowed unless approved by the building's juristic office. Most owners do not accept pets.

Regent Home Bangna or Ideo Mobi Eastgate?

Regent Home is newer and every unit has a separate bedroom at studio-level rents; Ideo Mobi Eastgate is closer to the BTS (about 300 m) on Sukhumvit Road.
