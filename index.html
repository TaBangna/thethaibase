Why Bang Na

Bang Na gives you more space for your rent than central Sukhumvit while staying on the same BTS line. Many of Bangkok's international schools sit along Bangna–Trad Road and Sukhumvit 105–107, and Suvarnabhumi Airport is a short drive east.
