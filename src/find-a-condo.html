How it works

  1. You send your requirements. It takes about a minute.
  2. We search. Our own listings, owners we know in each building, and trusted agents in the area.
  3. You get a shortlist on LINE or WhatsApp, with photos, rent and our honest notes, usually the same or next day.
  4. View in person or by video call. We go with you, in English or Thai.
  5. Sign and move in. We go through the lease and record the unit's condition with you.

No fee for renters. The owner pays our fee when a lease is signed.

Good to know

  • Most leases are 12 months, with a 2-month deposit and 1 month in advance.
  • Many owners ask foreign tenants for a passport and a work permit or visa.
  • Moving from abroad? We can do the viewings for you by live video.
