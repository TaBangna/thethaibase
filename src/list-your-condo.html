Rent out or sell your condo at BTS Bang Na

We focus on five buildings: The Coast Bangkok, Ideo O2, Ideo Mobi Eastgate, Ideo Mobi Eastpoint and Regent Home Bangna. Renters looking in this area come to one place to see every building, so your unit is in front of the people searching for it, Thai and foreign.

How it works

  1. Send us the building, unit size, bedrooms, asking rent and a few photos.
  2. We visit the unit and take photos for the listing.
  3. We list it on The Thai Base in Thai and English and match it to renters already looking in these five buildings.
  4. We screen enquiries and arrange viewings at times that suit you.
  5. We prepare the lease in Thai and English and record the unit's condition at move-in.

Fees

  • Listing is free, with no time limit.
  • We charge only when we find your tenant: one month's rent for a one-year lease.
  • For other lease lengths, we agree the fee with you in writing before we start.

Selling instead?

We list units for sale too, in the same five buildings. Send us the asking price, size, floor and photos and we'll put the unit on our for-sale page in Thai and English and bring buyers to view it. We agree our fee with you in writing before we start. Some owners list a unit for rent and for sale at the same time — that's fine.

What makes a unit rent quickly

  • Fully furnished, with a clear list of what's included
  • Good photos taken in daylight
  • Clean, with air conditioners serviced
  • A rent in line with similar units in the same building — see typical rents for each building
