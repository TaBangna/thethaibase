Rent your Bang Na condo to foreign tenants

We market your unit to expat professionals and families, show it, and handle the lease in English, so you get a reliable tenant without the back-and-forth.

How it works

  1. Send us the building, unit size, bedrooms, asking rent and a few photos.
  2. We visit the unit, take photos for the listing and agree our fee with you in writing.
  3. We list it on The Thai Base and share it with foreign renters looking in Bang Na.
  4. We screen enquiries and arrange viewings at times that suit you.
  5. We prepare the lease in Thai and English and record the unit's condition at move-in.

What makes a unit rent quickly

  • Fully furnished, with a clear list of what's included
  • Good photos taken in daylight
  • Clean, with air conditioners serviced
  • A rent in line with similar units in the same building
