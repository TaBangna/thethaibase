Living in Bang Na

Bang Na is on Bangkok's south-eastern edge, where Sukhumvit Road meets Bangna–Trad Road. It is quieter and roomier than central Sukhumvit, and it is where many international-school families choose to live.

Getting around

The BTS Sukhumvit line runs through the area with stations at Punnawithi, Udom Suk, Bang Na and Bearing, so you can reach Asok, Siam and the central business district without changing lines.

Bangna–Trad Road heads east toward Suvarnabhumi Airport and the industrial estates of the Eastern Seaboard. Traffic on it is heavy at rush hour, so check your commute at the time you'll actually travel.

International schools

The area is often called Bangkok's international school belt. Schools in and around Bang Na include:

  • Bangkok Patana School (Soi Lasalle)
  • St. Andrews International School (Sukhumvit 107)
  • Berkeley International School (Bangna–Trad Road)
  • Concordian International School
  • Wells International School (Bang Na campus)

Shopping and daily life

  • Central Bangna and Mega Bangna for groceries, shops and restaurants
  • 101 The Third Place near BTS Punnawithi
  • BITEC exhibition centre beside BTS Bang Na

Bangna Intersection Market (ตลาดสี่แยกบางนา)

For fresh food at local prices, this is the neighbourhood's big fresh market. It sells vegetables, fruit, meat, seafood, dry goods and ready-made Thai food, and is open every day from 5:00 to 22:00.

  • On Sanphawut Road, the same road as Regent Home Bangna. Enter from Soi Sanphawut 1 (by Wat Bang Na Nai).
  • Free parking around the market.
  • Go early in the morning for the best choice of fresh produce and seafood.

Hospitals

Private hospitals serving the area include Sikarin Hospital and Thainakarin Hospital. Larger international hospitals are in central Bangkok along the BTS line.
