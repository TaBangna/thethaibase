Tips for a short stay before you rent

  • Book a week first. Most people find a condo within one or two weeks of viewings. Extend the hotel if you need to.
  • Plan viewings before you land. Send us your budget and move-in date, and we line up units for your first days.
  • Check your visa. From 15 September 2026, visa-free stays are 30 days for most nationalities. See which visa fits your lease.
